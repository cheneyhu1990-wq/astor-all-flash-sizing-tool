How to use this tool: Filter or search the requirement library, then select the parameters relevant to your opportunity. The exported "Clean" text gives you customer-ready tender wording (English) for handoff to the account or bid team. The "with Internal Notes" export additionally includes the internal competitive-positioning rationale for each item — for internal review only, never for external distribution. Always validate current product capability with EDS Product Management (V5.3.0 guide) before finalizing a bid, since competitor capabilities and product versions change over time.
