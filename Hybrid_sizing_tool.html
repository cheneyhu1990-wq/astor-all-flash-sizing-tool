Licensing Guide:
• License capacity is evaluated on data disk raw capacity only (cache/SSD capacity is excluded).
• Required rule: Total Licensed Capacity ≥ Total Raw Capacity of all data disks in the cluster. Licenses can be stacked/combined.
• V5.0 Capacity license: cost-sensitive, high-capacity, lower performance priority. V5.0 Performance license: maximum performance, full-flash oriented.
• For converged deployment with block-storage-primary workloads on 1210W/1210-X5/1150W/1150-X5, the Capacity license is recommended (Performance license runs extra MDS services that compete for CPU).
